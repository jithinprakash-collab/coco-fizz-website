Where it began

Our story started with a simple question.

Before COCO FIZZ, our journey was rooted in a traditional coconut oil mill.

We made coconut oil using copra — the dried kernel of the coconut. During the process, the coconut water was simply being discarded. That made us pause and ask a simple question: could we make better use of what the coconut gives us?

That thought grew into an idea to explore beverages. It became COCO FIZZ — a new chapter alongside the roots of our coconut oil mill, bringing together coconut water and a range of fruit flavours.
